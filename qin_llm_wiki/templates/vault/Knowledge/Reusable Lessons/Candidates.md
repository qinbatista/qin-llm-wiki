# Auto-Classified Lesson Queue

This generated evidence queue contains bounded candidates from verified events. A candidate does not change a current rule until its evidence satisfies the classifier's promotion contract.

- Only explicitly global preferences may be promoted automatically. Repeated project outcomes remain review candidates until the user-selected model writes a reusable rule.
- Uncertain, one-off, sensitive, partially verified, or placeholder evidence is never promoted.
